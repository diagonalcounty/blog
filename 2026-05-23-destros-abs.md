---

**Elite Physique Journal:** Destro, your shredded midsection has been legendary since the original G.I. Joe animated series. Those steel-like abs look carved from the same material as your mask. How do you stay in peak condition while commanding a worldwide terrorist empire?

**Destro:** *metallic clink as he adjusts his mask* Evil doesn’t wait for soft bodies, lad. Every morning at 0500 — before Cobra Commander’s latest tirade — I’m in the fortress gym. One hundred hanging leg raises, dragon flags that test every fiber, and heavy cable crunches loaded with the weight of conquest. I train my core the way I forge alliances: unbreakable.

**Elite Physique Journal:** What’s your approach to nutrition as a high-level operative?

**Destro:** Precision fueling. High protein from the finest sources — ostrich, big game, captured rations when necessary. Complex carbs for sustained strategic planning sessions and greens for recovery. One calculated cheat meal per month, usually paired with single malt and the sweet taste of victory.

**Elite Physique Journal:** Balancing training, weapons R&D, and world domination sounds impossible. How do you manage it all?

**Destro:** Iron discipline and military scheduling. 5 AM core work. 6 AM heavy compounds — deadlifts, squats, presses — to keep the full frame imposing. By 0800 I’m designing destabilizers. A tight schedule produces a tight waist. No wasted effort. No room for weakness.

**Elite Physique Journal:** Advice for readers chasing “Destro-level” abs?

**Destro:** Train as if your empire depends on it — because it does. Progressive overload on every set. Visualize your enemy with each rep. The mask is optional. The abs are mandatory. Whether you fight for order or chaos, a warrior’s core is your ultimate weapon.

**Elite Physique Journal:** Will we ever see those abs in a modern reboot?

**Destro:** *dark chuckle* Only if the Joes dare challenge me again. Until then… stay hard, stay ruthless, and keep your midsection locked down tighter than Cobra security.
